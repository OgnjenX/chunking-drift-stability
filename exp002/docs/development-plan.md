# EXP002 bounded development plan (before exploratory runs)

EXP001 files and results are immutable. New code/results live under exp002/.

Development seeds: 101–103. Confirmatory seeds: 1001–1012, never used for tuning.
Development sequence families use AC/AD/BD and longer AC/AD-based lists; the
confirmatory families use AB/BC/CD and different longer orderings. Individual
symbols overlap because the circuit has four item channels; trial noise,
sequence order, and most trained lists do not. This is not a held-out species
or a large independent grammar sample.

First check exact equation derivatives, numerical convergence, bounds,
modulatory-feedback silence, size-dependent coefficients, and source-inspired
learning. Source replication is not claimed. The 2011 model plus 2014 learned
filters is a declared synthesis. Its limitations cannot be repaired by giving
correct list identities to hidden units or assigning template weights.

Bounded initial development: compare pulse durations .75, 2, and 6 model units,
with feedback 1 or 8. Use the source learning rule, initially rate .2; if it
changes filters by less than 1% in this finite budget, a separately logged rate
2 is allowed. These are simulation time units, not claimed biological seconds.
No confirmatory data may choose parameters. All attempted settings are saved.

Difficulty calibration uses delay, weak distractors, and input noise on NEW
held-out development trials. Choose a condition with intact and recurrent
control accuracy between .45 and .85, preferably .65, without selecting for a
chunk advantage. Record lighter/harder conditions and floors/ceilings. If no
such condition exists, freeze a failed-development report rather than pretend
headroom was achieved. Development mechanism diagnostics may motivate a
scientific stop, not a post-confirmation replacement.

Stage 1 tests meaningful learned selectivity, measurable competition/feedback
consequences, and retention of earlier items and short lists. An ordinary
trainable RNN receives the same raw sequential stimuli, labels, learning trials,
and delay/interference; it is not denied recurrence or an end-of-list signal.
An input-driven STORE2 reference is also evaluated. This is not a competition
against a deliberately memoryless control. Model parameter/exposure counts
and supervision differences must be reported.

Before confirmation freeze a separate protocol and all architecture/task
settings. Stage 2 and Stage 3 interpretation is prohibited unless every Stage 1
gate passes. Confirmation may consist of a failed Stage 1 and a justified stop.
A negative or development-only outcome must be retained and documented.
