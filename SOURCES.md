# Primary sources inspected for this replacement

Retrieved/inspected 2026-10-16.

- Kazerounian, S. & Grossberg, S. (2014). *Real-time learning of predictive
  recognition categories that chunk sequences of items stored in working
  memory*. Frontiers in Psychology 5:1053.
  [Publisher full text](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.01053/full).
  Sections 6.1–6.3, equations 12–13, and sections 7.1–7.2 distinguish prelearning
  selection from adaptive filter sharpening. The publisher's embedded MathML
  was inspected for the source-law equation. Only its isolated constant-input
  solution is reproduced; the coupled circuit is not validated.
- Carpenter, G. A. & Gjaja, M. N. (1993). *Fuzzy ART Choice Functions*.
  Boston University technical report CAS/CNS-93-060 (submitted WCNN 1994).
  [Institutional original](https://open.bu.edu/bitstreams/6089fca2-eb33-4168-9ded-daf4f68bc5b3/download).
  Algorithm section, equations 6–11, contains the choice, match and fuzzy-template
  update used by the independent primitive check. This is the actual document
  inspected, not the 1991 Carpenter/Grossberg/Rosen article or the 2014 circuit.

No third-party implementation was copied. Bibliographic facts and equations
should not be mistaken for empirical evidence that the new synthesis works.
See docs/mechanism.md for every source-to-model departure.
